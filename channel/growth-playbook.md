# Guilty Paws — Growth Playbook

Honest framing: nobody can guarantee YouTube outcomes — the algorithm is a
distribution lottery where quality and consistency buy more tickets. What this
playbook guarantees is that **no video fails for a reason we could have
controlled**: weak hook, broken metadata, inconsistent brand, missed trend, or
policy trouble.

## Phase roadmap

### Phase 0 — Foundation (now → first 1,000 total views)
**Goal: eliminate every self-inflicted handicap.**
- [ ] Fix the 5 launch titles in Studio (corrections in `logs/upload-log.md`).
- [ ] Paste the channel description from `brand-bible.md`; set country + channel keywords.
- [ ] Tick **"Altered content" (AI disclosure)** on all existing and future uploads.
- [ ] Name the dog (user decision) and update the brand bible.
- [ ] Authorize the channel in vidIQ so `vidiq_channel_analytics` unlocks.
- [ ] Start cross-posting every Short to TikTok + IG Reels.
- Cadence: 2/day, pillars rotating. Judge nothing yet — sample size is noise.

### Phase 1 — Signal hunting (→ ~100 subs)
**Goal: find the first repeatable winner.**
- Every video logged with hook type + pillar; weekly review compares them.
- A video with >10× the channel's median views = **breakout**: sequel within 48h,
  and its premise becomes a sub-series.
- Experiment deliberately: 1 in 4 videos tests a variable (length 12s vs 22s,
  narrator vs no narrator, caption vs no caption). One variable at a time,
  logged in the upload log, verdicts in weekly reviews.

### Phase 2 — Compounding (→ 1,000 subs)
- Double down on the top 2 pillars; retire the win-rate loser.
- Introduce numbered series + lore (see content-strategy).
- Community tab unlocks (500 subs): polls ("What should he destroy next?") —
  free engagement signal and free ideation.

### Phase 3 — Monetization (1,000 subs + 10M Shorts views/90d for YPP Shorts path)
- **The compliance work below is what makes this phase possible — read it.**
- Revenue diversification once eligible: Shorts ad-share is small per view;
  the real prizes at scale are brand deals (pet brands) and licensing.

## The algorithm, practically (Shorts, 2026)

1. Each Short gets a small test audience; **swipe-rate in the first ~2 seconds**
   decides whether it gets a bigger one. This is why Beat 1 of the format spec
   bans slow openings.
2. **Average % watched** is the dominant ranking signal — a 15s video watched
   1.3× loops beats a 40s video watched half-way. Stay ≤25s and engineer the loop.
3. Shorts distribution is bursty and delayed — videos can pop days or weeks
   after upload. Never delete or private a "flopped" Short; they are lottery
   tickets that stay in the draw.
4. Early engagement velocity (likes/comments in hour 1) correlates with wider
   testing. The pinned lore comment exists to bait first comments.
5. Consistent topicality builds channel-level identity: the more the channel is
   *only* about one dog's crimes, the better YouTube learns exactly who to
   show it to. Resist off-brand uploads entirely.

## Compliance & monetization risk (critical for an AI channel — do not skip)

- **Altered-content disclosure is mandatory.** YouTube requires marking
  realistic synthetic media. It does not hurt distribution; failing to mark it
  risks strikes. Tick it on every upload.
- **"Repetitious content" is the #1 YPP rejection risk for AI channels.**
  Mass-produced, near-identical AI clips get rejected at monetization review.
  Our defenses, which are also just good content: the deadpan narrator VO
  (original commentary), story structure (setup/escalation/punchline, not just
  a looping clip), recurring character + lore, and human-written titles/jokes.
  Keep all four; they are the difference between "AI slop" and "an animated
  comedy series that uses AI as the animation tool."
- **No real-animal-distress lookalikes.** Content that appears to show animal
  suffering gets age-restricted or removed even if synthetic. The brand bible's
  "dog is always delighted" rule is a policy shield, not just taste.
- Music: only YouTube Audio Library / generated tracks (`vidiq_generate_music`).
  One copyright claim on a Short kills its monetization and can throttle reach.

## KPI targets (review weekly; recalibrate monthly)

| Metric | Phase 0–1 target | Phase 2 target |
|---|---|---|
| Uploads/week | 14 | 14 |
| Median views (trailing 14 uploads) | establish baseline | 5× baseline |
| Avg % watched (once analytics unlock) | >85% | >95% |
| Subs per 1,000 views | >2 | >5 |
| Buffer of ready Shorts | ≥3 | ≥5 |

## Weekly rhythm

- **Mon:** `/weekly-review` (analytics-coach) → verdicts + this week's experiment.
- **Tue/Fri:** batch production days (`/produce-short` × 4–7).
- **Wed:** `/competitor-scan` + `/ideate` to refill the backlog to ≥15 concepts.
- **Daily (5 min):** community-manager pass on new comments; reply while small —
  early channels that reply to everything convert commenters into regulars.
