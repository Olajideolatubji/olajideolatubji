# Guilty Paws — Content Strategy

## Lanes (in priority order)

1. **Impossible Environments Indoors** ⭐ *priority lane — best performing per
   the user.* Weather and biomes where they cannot be: snow in the kitchen,
   rain in the living room, a beach in the hallway, fog in the bathroom, autumn
   leaves falling from the ceiling. At least **half of all concepts** come from
   this lane until the data says otherwise.
2. **Online Shopping Incidents** — absurd quantities of delivered things
   (10,000 ball pit balls is canon). Inherently escalating and sequel-friendly.
3. **Kitchen Crimes** — cooking attempts, food floods, appliance abuse.
4. **Home Renovation by Force** — couch excavations, TP blizzards, paint.
5. **Seasonal / Trend Hijacks** — the same premise mapped onto whatever is
   trending; sourced by trend-scout.

Every concept, regardless of lane, resolves into the canonical three shots:
disaster reveal (dog calm) → owner freezes → guilty side-eye push-in.

## Series mechanics (the subscriber engine)

Casual laughs get views; **continuity gets subscribers**:

- **The recurring cast** — same tan chihuahua, same owner, every video. The
  owner's freeze is a running gag in itself.
- **Pinned comment lore:** every video gets an in-universe pinned comment
  ("The kitchen has retained legal counsel."). Free engagement, trains viewers
  to open comments.
- **Numbered episodes** once the dog is named (`NAME's Disaster #23`).
- **Escalating sequels:** when a video outperforms, its premise gets a bigger
  sequel within 48h (snow kitchen → blizzard kitchen → the dog has built an
  igloo).
- If the format-change threshold is ever hit (see growth-playbook), the
  leading candidate is a **second recurring pet character** — per the user.

## Cadence & scheduling

- **2 Shorts/day**, 07:00 UTC and 17:00 UTC slots. Consistency is the
  strategy; protect it with a buffer of ≥3 ready-to-render prompt packages.
- Batch: write prompts for 4–7 videos in one session, render in Kling in one
  sitting, schedule the week.
- Consecutive uploads must come from different lanes — except sequels to a
  breakout, which override rotation.

## Distribution rules

- **Never reuse the same video across channels** (user rule — applies to any
  other channel or account, period).
- Each video lives on Guilty Paws only; growth comes from cadence, the loop,
  and lane focus — not from duplication.

## What we measure

- Views in first 24h/7d per video (upload log) — ask the user for Studio
  numbers or use public tools within credit budget.
- Lane win-rate — which lane's videos beat the channel median (tracked in the
  upload log; rebalances the concept mix monthly).
- Subs per 1,000 views once views exist — measures whether the *brand* lands.
- Milestone check at 15 uploads: if nothing has passed 50 views, trigger the
  format-change proposal per the growth playbook.
