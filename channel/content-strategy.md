# Guilty Paws — Content Strategy

## Content pillars (rotate; never two of the same pillar back-to-back)

1. **Kitchen Crimes** — cooking attempts, food floods, appliance abuse.
   (Launch examples: Italian cooking, popcorn kitchen.)
2. **Home Renovation by Force** — bathroom/living room transformations gone
   wrong. (Launch examples: bubbles bathroom, living-room aquarium.)
3. **Online Shopping Incidents** — the dog orders absurd quantities of things.
   (Launch example: 10,000 ball pit balls.) High-ceiling pillar: infinitely
   extensible and inherently escalating.
4. **The Investigation** *(series format)* — recurring "case file" framing:
   narrator reviews the evidence, dog remains a suspect. Builds lore and gives
   binge/playlist structure.
5. **Seasonal / Trend Hijacks** — the same premise mapped onto whatever is
   trending (holidays, viral sounds, news-safe events). Sourced by trend-scout.

## Series mechanics (the subscriber engine)

Casual laughs get views; **continuity gets subscribers**. Build these in:

- **Numbered crimes:** once the dog is named, title variant
  `NAME's Crime #23: ...` for pillar-4 videos.
- **Running gags:** the same personified victims recur (the long-suffering
  vacuum, the kitchen that "files for divorce" — already canon from launch).
- **Pinned comment lore:** every video gets a pinned comment in-universe
  ("The kitchen has retained legal counsel. Updates Friday."). Costs nothing,
  massively boosts comment engagement, trains viewers to open comments.
- **Cliffhanger loops:** occasionally end a Short with the *setup* of the next
  crime ("The package arriving tomorrow is bigger.").

## Cadence & scheduling

- **2 Shorts/day** at the established 07:00 UTC and 17:00 UTC slots. Hold this
  for at least 4 weeks before evaluating; the algorithm rewards consistency
  and 2/day gives 14 lottery tickets a week while the channel searches for its
  first breakout.
- Maintain a **ready buffer of ≥3 finished Shorts** at all times. Batch-produce
  on 1–2 production days/week rather than daily scrambling.
- If a video breaks out (VPH spikes, >10x median views), ship a **direct sequel
  within 24–48h** — same pillar, escalated premise. Breakouts are the channel's
  biggest growth events; sequels capture the audience the algorithm just found.

## Cross-platform (free distribution, do from day one)

The same 9:16 file posts natively to **TikTok** and **Instagram Reels** —
3 platforms for one production cost, and pet comedy travels identically on all
three. Watermark-free exports only (cross-posting watermarked videos is
suppressed). Use `vidiq_instagram_tiktok_outlier_search` to spot which premises
are breaking out on TikTok/IG before they hit YouTube — being 3 days early on a
trend is a structural advantage for a small channel.

## What we measure (see growth-playbook.md for targets)

- Swipe-through: % of impressions that stay past 3s (proxy until vidIQ channel
  auth is connected: views-per-hour trend in first 24h via `vidiq_video_stats`).
- Average % watched — the Shorts algorithm's core signal; >90% (looping >100%)
  is the goal, which is why length stays ≤25s.
- Subs per 1,000 views — measures whether the *brand* (not just the clip) lands.
- Pillar win-rate — tracked in the upload log; rebalance pillars monthly toward
  what wins.
