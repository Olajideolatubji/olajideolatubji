# Guilty Paws — Pre-Publish Quality Gate

**Every item must pass before a Short is recommended for upload. One failure =
fix or kill.** Ellis runs this as a literal ticked checklist at the end of
`/produce-short` and `/optimize-upload`.

## A. Video (Kling 3.0 output, reviewed by the user or Ellis if the file is shared)

- [ ] **15 seconds, 9:16, no watermark.**
- [ ] **Three-shot structure exact:** ① disaster reveal with the dog sitting
      calm (disaster legible in the first second — no build-up); ② owner (man
      in casual clothes) walks in and freezes; ③ slow push-in on the dog's
      guilty side-eye as it turns its head away.
- [ ] Final side-eye frame loops tolerably back into the shot-1 opening.
- [ ] Dog is on-model: tiny tan chihuahua, same look as every prior video; no
      warped anatomy in shot 1 or shot 3 (the two frames everyone sees).
- [ ] Owner is on-model: same man, casual clothes.
- [ ] Dog reads calm/serene — zero frames of apparent distress.
- [ ] Sound (Kling, sound on): disaster ambience in ①, door + silence beat in
      ②, comedic sting in ③; no copyrighted music.
- [ ] Watched start-to-finish twice at phone scale before approving.

## B. Metadata

- [ ] Title follows the canon rules: plain text, **no quotation marks**,
      **no trailing period**, **exactly one emoji**, ends with
      `#shorts #funnydog #chihuahua`, ≤70 chars before the hashtags.
- [ ] Title scored with `vidiq_score_title` when credits allow; best of ≥3
      candidates chosen.
- [ ] Description: one new deadpan joke line (not a title repeat), subscribe
      CTA, AI-generated-content line.
- [ ] Pinned comment drafted (in-universe lore or a question).

## C. Compliance

- [ ] **"Altered content" disclosure ticked** — stated explicitly in the
      delivery message, every single video.
- [ ] Nothing implies the footage is real.
- [ ] **This exact video is not posted on any other channel** (user rule).
- [ ] Made-for-kids setting: No (general-audience comedy); nothing
      age-restrictable.

## D. Strategy

- [ ] Lane differs from the previous upload's lane (unless it's a 48h breakout
      sequel).
- [ ] Scheduled into the next free 07:00 or 17:00 UTC slot (state which).
- [ ] If an experiment video: the single variable tested is named and logged.
- [ ] Upload-log row prepared in `channel/logs/upload-log.md`.

## Delivery format

When a package passes, deliver: the Kling prompts (or video file if rendered),
final title, description, pinned comment, schedule slot, the ticked checklist,
and the reminder: *"Tick Altered content in Studio when uploading."*
