---
name: competitor-scan
description: Refresh the competitive landscape for Guilty Paws — outlier videos, fast-growing pet-comedy channels, TikTok/IG early signals — and convert findings into backlog concepts. Use weekly or before planning a production batch.
---

Delegate to the **trend-scout** subagent with a budget cap: **≤20 vidIQ
credits** for the scan (it must check `vidiq_balance` and stay under).

Scope to request from it:
1. Outliers around the core niche (funny dog / guilty dog / chihuahua / AI dog
   comedy) — what overperformed in the last 2 weeks and *why mechanically*.
2. 3–5 comparable channels (faceless, pet comedy, ideally small-and-rising) —
   maintain them via `vidiq_update_competitors` / `vidiq_list_competitors` so
   the list persists between scans.
3. One TikTok/IG signal: a pet-comedy format breaking out there that hasn't
   saturated YouTube yet.
4. Every finding translated into a Guilty Paws concept in the brand's title
   grammar and appended to `channel/ideas-backlog.md` (scored, pillar-tagged).

Deliver the scout's report plus a one-line "so what": the single most
actionable thing to copy this week. If credits are too low to scan (<25
remaining), skip paid calls, say so, and run an ideation-only pass instead.
