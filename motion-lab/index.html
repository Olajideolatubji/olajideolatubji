The script

0 words

Plain text is fine. # makes the title, ## makes a chapter — chapters become on-screen markers and YouTube timestamps. Bullets become build-on lists. [NOTE: …] lines are treated as direction and never spoken.

Every duration in the build is measured from this. 150 is a normal read; 170 is fast; documentary narration runs nearer 130.

Holding scenes longer than the read is how a short script becomes a three-hour ambient piece. Past about 2× the preflight will say so.

The animation style

Each one is built around a different retention mechanic. The note under the name is what it is actually doing to hold the viewer — pick the one that matches how your script works, not the one that looks nicest.
